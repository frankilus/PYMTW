The story

Frank came to Bitcoin from a finance background — a Wall Street professional with an MBA and blockchain certification who has been studying and holding Bitcoin since 2016. What he kept noticing was a gap: the smartest, busiest people he knew were curious about Bitcoin but were served only two things — breathless hype or intimidating technical jargon. Neither helped a professional make a calm, responsible decision.

PYMTW — "Put Your Money to Work" — grew out of that gap. It's the resource Frank wished existed: honest, practical, and paced for people with careers, families, and limited patience for noise. The same thinking led to his books — Digital Credit: How Bitcoin Is Reinventing Yield and Bitcoin for Working Professionals.

Why PYMTW exists

Most people don't need to become traders, analysts, or engineers to hold Bitcoin responsibly. They need to understand what it is, build a simple accumulation process, and protect what they own — including planning for family access. PYMTW exists to make that path clear and doable.

Our education philosophy

  • Understanding before action — never the other way around
  • Process over prediction — discipline beats timing
  • Bitcoin-focused, not broadly "crypto"
  • Security and custody treated as first-class, not afterthoughts
  • No hype, no price targets, no fear-based selling

What PYMTW does

  • Teaches Bitcoin fundamentals clearly
  • Helps build disciplined, documented processes
  • Explains custody & security trade-offs
  • Provides checklists and frameworks you apply yourself

What PYMTW does not do

  • Manage assets or execute trades
  • Take custody of funds or control wallets
  • Give individualized investment, tax, or legal advice
  • Promise returns or predict Bitcoin's price

Security principles

PYMTW will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, passwords, authentication codes, or control of your funds. You keep sole control of your keys, accounts, and funds at all times. Read our full Security Promise.

Editorial standards

We aim to be accurate, current, and honest about uncertainty. We distinguish education from opinion, avoid sensational claims, and update material as facts change. Recommendations reflect merit for long-term holders — not compensation. Where we're not sure, we say so.
