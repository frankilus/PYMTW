Program FAQ

Is this financial or investment advice?
No. PYMTW is educational. We do not provide individualized investment, tax, or legal advice, recommend allocations, or tell you how much Bitcoin to buy or hold. See our Educational Disclaimer.
Will you ever handle my Bitcoin or ask for my keys?
Never. We do not take custody, execute trades, or control wallets, and we will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, passwords, or authentication codes. Read our Security Promise.
What's your refund policy?
Live workshops are refundable up to 48 hours before the session; digital products are covered by a 14-day satisfaction guarantee where the materials have not been substantially downloaded. Full details on our Policies page.
Are sessions recorded?
Workshops are recorded and shared with registrants. Private sessions can be recorded on request for your own use.
