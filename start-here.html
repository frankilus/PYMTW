Do I need to buy a whole bitcoin?

No. Bitcoin can be divided into smaller units. Understanding the unit is separate from deciding whether to buy any. Explore the full FAQ →

What does a Bitcoin wallet actually hold?

A wallet manages the keys used to authorize transactions; it does not contain physical coins. The ownership guide explains the difference between an exchange and self-custody. Read the ownership guide →

Is learning about Bitcoin the same as deciding to buy?

No. Start with education, consider risks and trade-offs, and take your time. The learning resources are useful whether or not you ever buy Bitcoin. Explore Evaluate →
