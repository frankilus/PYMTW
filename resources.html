Exchanges & buying platforms

Where you buy. Evaluate on fees, recurring-buy support, and whether you can withdraw to your own wallet. Availability and features vary by country.

Security consideration for all exchanges: funds held on an exchange are in someone else's custody. Enable app-based two-factor authentication (not SMS), and consider withdrawing longer-term holdings to self-custody.

Hardware wallets

For holding your own keys. Only ever buy direct from the manufacturer, and set up the device yourself.

Universal rule: the device generates your seed phrase — write it down offline, never type it into a computer or website, and never share it with anyone (including PYMTW). Learn the basics in our self-custody workshop.
