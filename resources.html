Wallet

Fully open-source, air-gap-capable, with an unusual "blind oracle" security model instead of a secure-element chip.

  • Who it's forBudget-conscious holders; open-source purists; DIY builders

Universal rule: the device generates your seed phrase — write it down offline, never type it into a computer or website, and never share it with anyone (including PYMTW). Learn the basics in our self-custody consultation.
