Who this is for

Holders who buy across multiple platforms and want to track cost basis and generate reports — alongside a qualified tax professional.

The criteria that matter

Exchange and wallet integrations

Can it import from the platforms and wallets you actually use?

Country and tax-method support

Confirm it supports your country's rules and accounting methods.

Report types

Does it produce the forms and summaries you (or your accountant) need?

Accuracy and review

Imports aren't perfect — you must review transactions for correctness.

Privacy

You're sharing transaction history — understand each tool's data handling.

Price tiers

Costs scale with transaction count; confirm current tiers.

Advantages and limitations

Advantages

  • Automates cost-basis tracking across platforms
  • Generates reports for you or your accountant
  • Saves time and reduces year-end scramble

Limitations

  • Imports need review for accuracy
  • Shares your transaction history with a third party
  • Software is not a substitute for a tax professional

Which tax tool is best?
The one that supports your country and the platforms you use, at a price that fits your transaction count. Confirm current details before subscribing.
Is this tax advice?
No. It's educational. Consult a qualified tax professional for your situation. See our disclaimer.
Do I even need software?
If you buy on one platform infrequently, a simple log may be enough. Software helps most when you use several platforms or trade often.
