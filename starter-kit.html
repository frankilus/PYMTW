What it produces

A binder your family can actually use

Answer plain-English questions on screen, then print the result. The binder is meant to sit with your other important papers.

Personal Bitcoin policy

Why you hold Bitcoin, your time horizon, and the rules you’ve set for yourself, written down before headlines test them.

Executor letter

A plain letter for the person who may one day need to act: what exists, who to call, and what not to do.

Wallet inventory, no secrets

Where your Bitcoin lives and how it is organized, without seed phrases, private keys, or passwords.

Private by design: the plan runs in your browser, so your answers stay on your device. Only a license key you enter is checked with Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. It never asks for your seed phrase, private keys, or passwords, and you should never share them with anyone, including PYMTW.
