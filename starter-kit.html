The problem

Owning Bitcoin is easy. Staying organized isn't.

Most people buy some Bitcoin and then leave the important parts to memory — no buying plan, no records, no backup strategy, no plan for what happens if something goes wrong. Years later, that disorganization is what causes real losses. This kit turns "I'll figure it out later" into a simple, documented system you can finish in a weekend.
