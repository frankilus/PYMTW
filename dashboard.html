Methodology & Disclaimer
Data Sources: Fear & Greed Index from Alternative.me. BTC price, dominance, and historical data from CoinGecko. On-chain metrics (MVRV Z-Score, NUPL, SOPR, Realized Price, Supply in Profit, Puell Multiple) from BGeometrics API with manual fallback values. Network data (mempool, fees, difficulty, Lightning) from Mempool.space. Corporate treasury data from CoinGecko and SEC filings. ETF flow data from public aggregators. M2 Money Supply and PMI from DBnomics (Federal Reserve, ISM). Cycle models use well-established regression coefficients. MSTR data from quarterly SEC filings.

Signal Methodology: Each metric produces a signal (Accumulate, Hold, or Caution) based on established threshold zones. The overall signal is a weighted average of all individual metric signals, with cycle indicators (MVRV, NUPL, Rainbow) receiving the highest weight, followed by sentiment and technical indicators, then macro environment.
