Common questions

How long will my savings last if I lose my job?

Divide the money you can draw on by what you spend each month. Counting severance and unemployment benefits, and trimming expenses, can extend that number.

Should I count unemployment benefits in my runway?

If you are likely eligible, yes, but amounts and duration depend on your state. File as soon as you can and use your state’s estimate rather than a guess.

What should I do first after losing my job?

File for unemployment, check your final paycheck and severance terms, and sort out health coverage. Our Just lost your job guide walks through each step.
