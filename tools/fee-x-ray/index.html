Common questions

What is an expense ratio?

It is the annual fee a fund charges, shown as a percentage of the money you have invested. It is taken out of the fund’s returns, so you never see a bill.

Where do I find my fund’s expense ratio?

On the fund’s fact sheet or prospectus, or on your brokerage or 401(k) provider’s website.

Is a 1% advisory fee a lot?

It can be. Because it is charged on your whole balance every year, the dollar cost grows with your account. Use this tool to see what it would cost on your numbers and weigh that against the service you get.

Do fees really matter if returns are good?

Yes. Fees are subtracted whether returns are good or bad, and the money lost to fees also stops compounding for you.
