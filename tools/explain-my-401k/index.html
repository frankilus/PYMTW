Common questions

How do I find the fees in my 401(k)?

Look for your plan’s fee disclosure and each fund’s expense ratio, usually on your provider’s website or in the fund fact sheet.

Am I getting my full employer match?

Check your plan’s match formula and compare it with your contribution rate. Contributing less than the match leaves part of it unclaimed.

What happens to my 401(k) if I leave my job?

You can usually leave it in the plan, roll it to an IRA or a new employer’s plan, or cash it out. Cashing out is generally taxable and may face an extra 10% tax before age 59½.
