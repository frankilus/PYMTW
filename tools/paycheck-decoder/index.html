How this works

The decoder subtracts the tax and deduction amounts you enter from cash gross pay, then adds cash payments not already included in gross. It compares that result with the optional net-pay figure. It does not infer taxable wages, apply tax tables, or assess annual withholding. Special cases such as stock compensation, non-cash benefits and payroll reversals may need help from payroll.

Common questions

Does my pay stub leave my device?

No. Photo and PDF reading runs in this browser tab. Your file and extracted amounts are not sent to a server or saved in browser storage.

Can I correct imported amounts?

Yes. Compare the suggested lines with your original pay stub, select current-period amounts and correct any mistakes before importing. Imported entries remain editable.

Does this calculate my tax bill?

No. It explains the amounts you enter and checks whether gross cash pay minus deductions and taxes, plus cash additions, matches your reported net pay. It does not estimate annual tax liability.
