Decode the lines

Add each employee tax and deduction once. Select what the line represents to see its explanation. For reimbursements and employer-paid items, choose the matching type.

Do not add “Total deductions” if you have entered the individual lines. Amounts must be zero or positive. Use “Cash addition outside gross pay” for a refund that increases this check.

This checks arithmetic using your entries. It does not verify payroll accuracy or calculate how much tax you should owe.

MAKE SENSE OF THE SMALL PRINT

What does that code mean?

Look up common entries even if you do not have your pay stub handy. Employers use different codes, so confirm unfamiliar labels with payroll.

No matching term. Ask payroll to explain the code, or record it as “Other deduction / not sure” if it reduces your cash pay.

How this works

The decoder subtracts the tax and deduction amounts you enter from cash gross pay, then adds cash payments not already included in gross. It compares that result with the optional net-pay figure. It does not infer taxable wages, apply tax tables, or assess annual withholding. Special cases such as stock compensation, non-cash benefits and payroll reversals may need help from payroll.
