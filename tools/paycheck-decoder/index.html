Common questions

What is FICA on my paycheck?

FICA is the combined Social Security and Medicare tax taken from most wages. Your employer pays a matching share.

What is the difference between pre-tax and post-tax deductions?

Pre-tax deductions, such as traditional 401(k) contributions and many health premiums, come out before income tax is calculated and lower your taxable pay. Post-tax deductions come out after tax.

How do I know if my withholding is too high or too low?

Compare what is being withheld with what you expect to owe for the year. The IRS Tax Withholding Estimator can help, and you can change withholding with a new Form W-4.
