Common questions

What is cost basis for bitcoin?

It is generally what you paid to acquire the bitcoin, including certain fees. Your gain or loss when you sell is the sale proceeds minus that basis.

Why do I need my records from every exchange?

If you moved coins between exchanges or wallets, one platform may not know what you originally paid. Combining records helps keep the original basis attached to each lot.

Is this tax advice?

No. Crypto tax rules can be complex and change over time. Confirm your situation with a tax professional or official IRS guidance.
