Common questions

Is it cheaper to rent or buy a home?

It depends. Buying tends to make more sense the longer you stay, because upfront costs are spread over more years. Renting can win over shorter stays or where prices are high relative to rents.

What costs of owning do people forget?

Property tax, homeowners insurance, maintenance and repairs, HOA dues, PMI if you put down less than 20% on a conventional loan, and closing costs when you buy and sell.

Why does the calculator include investment returns?

Money used for a down payment could otherwise be invested. Including that opportunity cost makes the comparison fair to renting.

How long do I need to stay for buying to make sense?

There is no single answer. Try different time horizons here; the point where buying pulls ahead depends on your prices, rates and assumptions.
