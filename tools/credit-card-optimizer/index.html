Common questions

Is a cash back card or a travel card better?

It depends on your spending and whether you will use the points. A flat cash back card is simple; category and travel cards can earn more if your spending fits their bonus categories and the rewards beat the annual fee.

Is a card with an annual fee worth it?

Only if the extra rewards you would actually earn are larger than the fee. This tool subtracts annual fees so you can compare net rewards.

Should I chase rewards if I carry a balance?

Usually not. Interest on a carried balance typically outweighs rewards, so paying the card off first tends to come out ahead.

Does this tool recommend specific cards?

No. It compares common card types, not specific offers. Check the current terms of any card before you apply.
