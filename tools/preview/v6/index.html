THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Are these financial tools really free?

Yes. Every PYMTW tool and guide is free to use, with no signup, no paywall and no ads.

Do my numbers leave the browser?

No. The calculators run entirely in your browser, so the numbers and files you enter stay on your device. The site counts anonymous page visits with GoatCounter, and if you join the newsletter, the signup form is run by beehiiv, which receives the email address you enter.

Which tool should I start with?

If you are not sure, take the 2-minute money checkup. It asks a few questions and gives you a short, prioritized list of what to look at, with links to the right tools.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools are educational and give estimates based on what you enter and the assumptions shown. For decisions about your situation, talk to a licensed professional.
