🗺️ New · Planning

Your money, on one timeline.

Add a few numbers and the goals you care about. See when you’ll reach each one, then drag the sliders to see what moves them.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
First, a little about you.

Your age sets the timeline. Income is used only for the retirement rule of thumb.

What do you have saved?

Rough numbers are fine.

What do you owe?

Credit cards, car and personal loans, added together. Leave out your mortgage. Enter 0 if none.

How much can you put away each month?

Savings after bills and debt payments, plus what goes into retirement (count any employer match).

Which goals matter to you?

Pick any. Add a target year so we can tell you if you’re on track.

Educational only, not financial advice. This is a simple projection, not a forecast. Amounts are in today’s dollars and the return is an assumption you control (after inflation), not a prediction. Cash goals are assumed to earn nothing. Savings fill your emergency fund first, then your other goals one at a time in order of target year; once debt is paid off, that payment goes to savings; anything left goes to retirement. The default retirement target uses Fidelity’s rule of thumb of saving 10× your salary by 67 (Fidelity). Your own target may differ. Nothing you enter leaves your device.
