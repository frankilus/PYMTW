Common questions

How much should I have in an emergency fund?

A widely used rule of thumb is three to six months of essential expenses. People with variable income, one earner in the household or less stable jobs often aim higher.

Should I count all my expenses or just essentials?

For runway, essentials are what matter: housing, food, utilities, insurance, transportation and minimum debt payments. You would likely cut extras in an emergency.

Where should I keep my emergency fund?

Somewhere safe and easy to reach, such as an insured high-yield savings account, rather than invested in assets that can drop in value right when you need them.
