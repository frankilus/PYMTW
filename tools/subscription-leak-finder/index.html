Common questions

How do I find all my recurring subscriptions?

Download a few months of transactions as a CSV from your bank or card website, then load it here. Charges from the same merchant that repeat on a regular schedule are flagged as recurring.

Is it safe to upload my bank statement?

The file never leaves your device. It is read and analyzed by JavaScript in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Why didn’t it catch one of my subscriptions?

Detection needs the charge to repeat in the data, so a single month of history or an annual subscription may not show up. Use a longer date range if you can.

What should I do once I find a subscription I don’t use?

Cancel it with the merchant directly, then check your next statement to confirm the charge stopped.
