    Company account or self-custody: what’s the difference?

    At an exchange or brokerage, a company holds the Bitcoin and your family works with its support team, like a bank. In self-custody, you hold it with a wallet device or app, and a backup (often 12 or 24 words) can restore it. Whoever has that backup can take the Bitcoin, which is why this binder only says where it is.

    We leave balances out on purpose. A printed list of amounts is a tempting target.

    Who should your family call?

    Name the person in charge and the people who can help. Professionals like an estate attorney or CPA can handle legal ownership, taxes and authority. A trusted helper can sit beside your family, not take the devices away.

    Do my executor’s rights cover digital accounts?

    Most U.S. states have adopted a version of a uniform law on fiduciary access to digital assets. It affects what companies can share with an executor. Ask your estate attorney how it applies to you, and whether your will should mention digital assets.

    If I cannot act

    During illness or incapacity, ask the appropriate professional to confirm who has authority; an executor’s role after death is different. After death, follow the estate process and provider requirements. This binder grants neither access nor authority.

    Match the instructions to the holding

    • Exchange account: use the provider’s verified estate or authorized-representative process.
    • Bitcoin ETF: this is a brokerage holding, not a wallet to recover. Follow the broker’s process.
    • Self-custody: keep recovery instructions separate and secure; do not experiment with transfers.
    • Multisig: identify the coordinator and document where the separate recovery procedure is held. Do not bring keys together in this binder.

    Why write a Bitcoin plan at all?

    Bitcoin you control yourself can be lost for good if nobody knows it exists or where the backup is. Bitcoin at a company can sit unclaimed if nobody knows which company. A clear, current document helps your family discover accounts and locate the right support, and gives you a steady rulebook for the days the price makes headlines.

    Why not just write down my recovery words for my family?

    Anyone who sees those words can take the Bitcoin, and a binder can be seen by many people over the years. Keep the backup itself secure and separate, and use this binder to say where it is and who can help. Your attorney can advise on secure ways to pass on access, such as sealed instructions or a collaborative custody setup.

    Is this a will?

    No. Your will and estate documents decide who inherits. This binder helps the people you trust find things and avoid mistakes. Review it with an estate attorney.
