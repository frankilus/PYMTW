Common questions

What is dollar-cost averaging?

It means investing a fixed amount on a regular schedule, such as monthly, no matter the price. You buy more when prices are low and less when they are high.

Where does the price data come from?

Monthly averages from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED): the Coinbase Bitcoin series for bitcoin and the S&P 500 price index, which excludes dividends.

Does a good backtest mean bitcoin will keep going up?

No. A backtest only shows what happened in the past. Bitcoin has been highly volatile, and future returns may be very different.

Why do my results differ from my exchange account?

The tool uses monthly average prices and ignores fees, taxes and exact purchase dates, so real results will differ.
