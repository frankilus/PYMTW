Common questions

Is the avalanche or snowball method better?

Avalanche usually saves the most interest because it targets the highest rate first. Snowball can be easier to stick with because small balances disappear sooner. The best method is the one you will keep doing.

How is my debt-free date calculated?

The planner applies your monthly payment to your listed balances and rates month by month until every balance is paid. It assumes rates and payments stay the same.

Should I pay off debt or save first?

Many people keep a small emergency cushion while paying down high-interest debt, so a surprise bill does not go back on a card. The right balance depends on your rates and job security.

Does paying extra really make a difference?

Usually yes. Extra payments go straight to principal, which reduces future interest and can move your debt-free date noticeably. Enter different payment amounts to see the effect.
