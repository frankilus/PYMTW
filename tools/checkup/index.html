Common questions

How long does the money checkup take?

About two minutes. The questions come one at a time and most are multiple choice.

Do I get a financial score?

No. Instead of a score, you get a short, prioritized list of blind spots with links to tools that can help.

Are my answers saved?

Only if you tick “Remember my answers,” and then only in this browser. Otherwise nothing is kept after you leave.

Is the checkup financial advice?

No. It uses general rules of thumb that may not fit your situation. Treat the results as a starting point, not a recommendation.
