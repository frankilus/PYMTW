Common questions

Are PYMTW Tools really free?

Yes. Every tool and guide is free to use, with no signup and no ads.

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculators run entirely in your browser, so the numbers and files you enter stay on your device. The site counts anonymous page visits to improve the tools.

Which tool should I start with?

If you are not sure, take the 2-minute money checkup. It asks a few questions and gives you a short, prioritized list of what to look at, with links to the right tools.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools are educational and give estimates based on what you enter. For decisions about your situation, talk to a licensed professional.
