A few things to know.

How do I use the tools?

Search in your own words or choose a topic, open a tool, and enter your numbers. Review its assumptions and limits, then explore how different choices affect the estimate.

Are PYMTW Tools really free?

Yes. Every calculator and guide is free to use, with no signup and no ads. The one exception is the Bitcoin household plan: it is free to build, and printing its binder is a one-time $29.

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculators run entirely in your browser, so the numbers and files you enter stay on your device. The Bitcoin household plan sends nothing but a license key you enter, which is checked with Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. The site counts anonymous page visits to improve the tools.

Which tool should I start with?

If you are not sure, take the 2-minute money checkup. It asks a few questions and gives you a short, prioritized list of what to look at, with links to the right tools.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools are educational and give estimates based on what you enter. For decisions about your situation, talk to a licensed professional.
