The true cost of a wedding. Plan the day, protect the years after.

Where wedding money goes, a budget calculator built around your guest list, and how to pay for it without starting married life in debt. Every figure links to its source.

    Paying for it

    Protect the years after the wedding.

    5

    What the average leaves out

    The Knot lists an engagement ring averaging $4,600, rehearsal dinner $2,800 and honeymoon $5,500 outside the ceremony-and-reception total. Its advice includes a just-in-case buffer around 5%.1 Zola reports about $1,100 for pre-wedding beauty, fitness and wellness, often outside the budget.3 Check local license and ceremony requirements; a large celebration is optional.

    6

    Agree on who pays early

    Zola found 88% of couples contribute and 71% receive family help; among those, 69% have contributions from both families.3 The Knot’s averages vary with who pays: $39,600 when the couple pays a minority, $29,700 when they pay most and $25,500 when they pay all.1 Agree on amounts and expectations before booking. Do not treat hoped-for gifts as confirmed funds.

    7

    Keep other goals in the conversation

    Zola reports 52% of couples paused other milestones. Among the goals deferred were general savings (70%), a home (59%), debt payoff (38%) and starting a family (29%). It also reports 4% took a loan.3 Look at the homeownership guide and baby guide before deciding what fits.

    8

    If you are considering a card or loan

    The Federal Reserve’s August 2026 average for credit cards assessed interest was 22.36%; the commercial-bank average for a 24-month personal loan was 11.90%.4 The calculator compares a fixed balance with equal monthly payments, no new spending and no fees. Actual card minimum payments can take much longer.

    Common questions

    What is the average cost of a wedding?

    The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study, which surveyed 10,474 couples married in 2025, puts the average at $34,200 and the median at $24,000. Zola’s survey of couples planning 2026 weddings puts the average at $36,000. Location, season and guest count drive most of the difference.

    Source 1

    How much does a wedding cost per guest?

    About $292 per guest, according to The Knot, with an average of 117 guests. Weddings with 1 to 50 guests averaged $17,100, 51 to 100 guests $27,200, and more than 100 guests $43,300.

    Source 1

    What is the most expensive part of a wedding?

    The reception venue, at $12,900 on average, according to The Knot. Catering averaged $80 per person, a photographer $3,000 and a live band $4,500.

    Source 1

    What costs are not included in the average wedding cost?

    The Knot’s average leaves out the engagement ring ($4,600 on average), the rehearsal dinner ($2,800) and the honeymoon ($5,500). Zola found couples also spend about $1,100 on beauty and wellness before the day. Check local marriage-license and ceremony requirements; a large celebration is optional.

    Source 1

    Should we use a credit card or loan to pay for our wedding?

    It can cost a lot. The average credit card rate on accounts charged interest was 22.36% in August 2026, according to the Federal Reserve. $10,000 at that rate, paid off over 24 months, is about $2,493 in interest. If you borrow, borrow only a set amount you can repay on a schedule, and compare APRs and fees first.

    Source 4

    How do we talk about money before getting married?

    The FDIC suggests sharing your incomes, debts and credit reports, deciding whether you will use joint accounts, separate accounts or both, and building a shared budget that includes savings for unexpected costs.

    Source 5
