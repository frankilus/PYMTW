COBRA or the Marketplace? Health coverage after a job loss.

You don’t have to take COBRA. You get at least 60 days to elect it, and losing job-based coverage also gives you a 60-day window to pick a Marketplace plan, so price both before either clock runs out.25 COBRA keeps the plan you have, usually at the full premium plus 2%; a Marketplace plan may cost less if you qualify for savings based on your income.16 This sheet has 34 items in 4 sections, a deadline sheet and a side-by-side table to fill in, plus questions to ask HR. Each item links to an official source, and each section prints on its own page.

Open Enrollment runs November 1 to January 15. For 2027 coverage on HealthCare.gov, enroll by December 15, 2026 for a January 1 start, or by January 15, 2027 for a February 1 start. Outside that window, you need a Special Enrollment Period, such as losing job-based coverage.8

  1. On what exact date does my job-based health coverage end?
  2. When will the COBRA election notice be sent, and to what address?
  3. What is the full monthly COBRA premium for my coverage (just me, me plus spouse, or family)?
  4. Will the company pay any part of my COBRA premium? For how many months, and can you confirm that in writing?
  5. Who administers COBRA, how do I pay, and when is each payment due?
  6. How much have I paid toward this year’s deductible and out-of-pocket maximum?
  7. When does the plan year end, and when does the deductible reset?
  8. Will my current doctors, hospitals and prescriptions be covered the same way under COBRA?
  9. Can I elect COBRA for dental or vision separately from medical?
  10. On what date would my COBRA coverage end if I keep it for the full period?
  11. Is our plan subject to federal COBRA? If not, is there state continuation coverage?
  12. Can you send a letter confirming the date my coverage ends, in case the Marketplace asks for proof?

Common questions

Is COBRA or a Marketplace plan cheaper?

It depends on your plan and your income. With COBRA you usually pay the full premium plus 2%. A Marketplace plan may cost less, especially if you qualify for savings based on your household income for the year. Compare total yearly costs, not just premiums.2612

How long do I have to decide?

You get at least 60 days to elect COBRA, from when coverage ends or when you get the election notice, whichever is later. For a Marketplace plan, you can pick a plan from 60 days before to 60 days after your job-based coverage ends.237

Can I switch from COBRA to a Marketplace plan later?

Yes during Open Enrollment, November 1 to January 15. Outside it, only if your COBRA is running out, your former employer stops paying its share, you’re still within 60 days of losing job-based coverage, or you have another life event. Voluntarily dropping COBRA doesn’t qualify.6

When is Open Enrollment for 2027 coverage?

November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027 on HealthCare.gov. Enroll by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1.8

Does my severance affect Marketplace savings?

Savings are based on your household’s expected income for the whole calendar year, including what you earned before you left. Severance pay is taxable income, so include it in your estimate.51117

What if my employer has fewer than 20 employees?

Federal COBRA generally doesn’t apply, but many states have “mini-COBRA” laws that may cover smaller employers. Ask HR, or check with your state insurance commissioner’s office.12
