🖨️ Printable review sheet

Handed a severance agreement? Check these first.

Before you sign, find your deadline, read what you’re giving up, and get every number and date in writing. If you’re 40 or older, federal rules give you at least 21 days to decide (45 in a group layoff) and 7 days to change your mind after signing.12 This sheet has 46 items in 5 sections, plus questions to ask HR. Each item links to an official source, and each section prints on its own page.

This is not legal advice. It’s a plain-English list of what to check, based on federal rules and official guidance. State law and your agreement’s exact words matter, so have an employment lawyer review it before you sign. Find one through your state or local bar’s lawyer referral service, or free legal aid if you qualify.

Questions to ask HR

Copy these into your own email or notes. Nothing is sent from this page.

  1. What is the exact deadline to sign, and is this your final offer?
  2. How was my severance calculated, and when and how will it be paid (lump sum or installments)?
  3. Will my unused vacation or PTO be paid out, and is it separate from severance?
  4. Will I get any earned or prorated bonus and unpaid commissions, and when?
  5. What happens to my unvested stock, options or RSUs, and what is my exercise deadline?
  6. What is my vested 401(k) balance, and does a vesting date fall soon after my last day?
  7. On what date does my health coverage end, and will the company pay any part of COBRA?
  8. Can I convert my group life or disability insurance, and what is the deadline?
  9. How will my departure be described: layoff, position elimination or resignation?
  10. What will the company say to reference checks, and who will answer them?
  11. Is this part of a group program? If I’m 40 or older, please send the job titles and ages disclosure.
  12. Which personal records may I keep, and can you confirm that in writing?
