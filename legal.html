Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to provide education and communicate with you:

  • Email & name when you subscribe, download a resource, take the assessment, or make an inquiry. These are handled by our email provider (Beehiiv) to deliver newsletters and resources.
  • Basic, privacy-respecting analytics about page visits and button clicks to understand what's useful. We configure analytics to exclude sensitive financial fields (see Calculator Privacy).

We do not sell your personal information. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any message. To request access to or deletion of your information, contact us.

We never ask for and never want your seed phrase, private keys, passwords, authentication codes, bank or brokerage account numbers, or government identification through this website.

Calculator Privacy

The Net-Worth Calculator, DCA Calculator, and Performance tools run entirely in your web browser. The numbers you enter are used only to compute results on your device.

  • Local by design: your entered financial values are processed in your browser and are not transmitted to PYMTW or stored on our servers.
  • Not sent to analytics: our analytics are configured to exclude the financial values you type. We may record that a calculator was used, but not the amounts.
  • Live market data may be fetched from public price APIs to power calculations; these requests do not include your personal figures.
  • Please do not enter account numbers, passwords, or any account-identifying information into any calculator. It is never required.
