The initial deposit buys at the selected starting month’s sample price. Monthly deposits begin one month later, including the ending month; one year means 12 monthly deposits after the initial deposit. Valuation uses the last included sample price. Fees, spreads, taxes and inflation are excluded. The lump-sum comparison assumes all contributed cash was available at the start. This is an educational illustration, not a forecast.

Illustrative data covers January 2011–March 2026. The original source and sampling method were not documented, so these values must not be treated as verified monthly closing prices or used to establish actual investment performance. Download the exact price series (CSV).

How to read this. Dollar-cost averaging spreads purchases over time so no single entry price defines your outcome. The average buy price above shows the discipline effect; the growth chart shows how bumpy the ride was along the way. Use this to build a realistic mental model — not to predict returns.
Volatility & drawdown reminder. Bitcoin has repeatedly fallen 50%–80% from its highs and taken years to recover. Any period you select could have included long, deep drawdowns. Illustrative results are hypothetical and do not predict future performance. Only you can decide what, if anything, fits your situation.
Your privacy: this calculator runs entirely in your browser. The amounts and dates you enter are not transmitted to PYMTW, not stored, and never sent to analytics. Only public Bitcoin price data is fetched. Calculator Privacy policy.
